# Neuromorph

Vascular surgery app and Nature-style preprints — quantum kernel, soft-tissue
and finite element modelling, catheter performance optimization, and health
economics. Independent work, not peer-reviewed. All results are synthetic-only,
with no patient data and no quantum advantage claimed. Manufacturer archetypes
are generic teaching models, not real product specs.

## Papers (`papers/`)

- `quantum-kernel-soft-tissue-modelling-preprint.pdf` — v1.2, 26 pages.
  "Quantum kernel scoring coupled to finite hyperelastic soft-tissue modelling
  for vascular surgery." 6-qubit angle-encoding quantum kernel, finite
  hyperelastic soft-tissue model, finite element simulation of balloon/stent
  expansion (§10, with catheter-design implications in §10.6), and health
  economics with a 3-year revenue trajectory and statistical ML cross-check
  (§11, scale scenario $108.09M in Year 3).
- `qml-pharmacogenomic-clinical-trial-preprint.pdf` — v1.1, 14 pages.
  "Quantum machine learning signatures for pharmacogenomic states: a
  finite-mathematics framework and adaptive clinical trial management
  protocol", with Part II health economics for Sunnybrook Oncology's Odette
  Cancer Centre over 2027–2036. Independent scenario model, not a Sunnybrook
  forecast.
- `boston-scientific-catheter-supply-chain-qml-preprint.pdf` — v1.0, 11 pages.
  "Quantum machine learning for catheter supply chains: elliptic-integral
  seasonality and harmonic modular-form features." Independent, no Boston
  Scientific affiliation.
- `reconciliation-draft.md` — draft note reconciling the two different
  >$100M Year-3 revenue models (app service-line $101.7M vs preprint
  platform $108.09M). Different models, not the same projection.
- `extension-v1.1-draft.md` / `boston-scientific-extension-v1.1-draft.md` —
  unbuilt v1.1 extension drafts.

## App (`app/`)

The Vascular Surgery App (v7) is a hosted fullstack app. See
`app/README.md` for its tabs, models, and verified numbers. Its source is
not a local folder in this repo; this package is a snapshot of its
deliverables and documentation, prepared for pushing alongside the papers.
